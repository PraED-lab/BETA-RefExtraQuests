# 🎯 Ref Extra Quests 1.5.0

**68 extra quests for Ref (the Arena trader) in SPT 4.1.x.** A long, level-gated quest line from level 10 to 50, with texts in 16 languages.

![version](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-1.5.0-blue) ![SPT](https://img.shields.io/badge/SPT-4.1.x-green) ![license](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-lightgrey)

## ✨ What you get
- **68 quests** for Ref: the original 24-quest chain plus **44 new quests** added in 1.4.0.
- **Level 10 to 50.** The first quest is available from level 10, with no Ref prerequisite.
- **One quest line per map** (6 quests each): Customs, Woods, Shoreline, Reserve, Lighthouse and Streets of Tarkov. All lines unlock after the first quest, so you pick the order.
- **8 "special orders"** playable on any map: headshots, long-range kills (50 m+), night kills, repeated extractions and a grand finale.
- **Original chain** (24 quests): Scav, BEAR, USEC and PMC hunts, Ground Zero, Factory and Interchange, extractions.
- Rewards: XP, roubles, dollars or euros, GP coins and Ref reputation, scaled to the quest level.

## 🌍 Languages
English, Français, Русский, Deutsch, Español (ES + LATAM), 中文, 日本語, 한국어, Polski, Português, Čeština, Türkçe, Italiano, Magyar, Română, Slovenčina.

The mod uses your SPT language automatically. English is the fallback.

> Translations are AI-made and not reviewed by native speakers. Spotted a mistake? Open an issue, corrections are welcome.

## 📦 Requirements
- SPT 4.1.x
- [acidphantasm-reffriendlyquests](https://forge.sp-tarkov.com)

## 🔧 Installation
Copy the `SPT_Runtime` folder into your SPT folder (it merges with the existing one):

```
SPT_Runtime/user/mods/RefExtraQuests/
```

**Updating from an older version:** replace the `RefExtraQuests` folder. Quest IDs are unchanged.

## ⚠️ Before you play
Back up your profile first (`SPT/user/profiles/<your_id>.json`). Once a quest is accepted it is written in the profile, so do not remove the mod afterwards.
